Together with Loyiso, which is DJ Loyd’s first name, I grew up in the township of Khayelitsha. We always wanted to create a style of music that would be relevant to people in our neighborhood, but also in all other parts of the city. I believe that music has the power to break down barriers and bring people from all parts of society together. We are all one!
