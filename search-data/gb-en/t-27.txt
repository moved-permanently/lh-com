["Discover the best things to do in Cagliari at a glance, with tips on food, sights and experiences for an inspiring stay.","Cagliari is located on the south coast of Sardinia. The capital of the island is characterized by its proximity to the Mediterranean Sea and extensive history, which is tangible when strolling through the alleyways of the old town. If you are looking for authentic Italian life, you will find it in Cagliari, where the people are open and laid-back and very warm. Marvel at the flamingos that live in the city, admire the Roman amphitheater – or just let yourself drift and see what the day has in store Fregola is a Sardinian specialty made from roasted durum wheat semolina formed into small balls. Roasting gives it a nutty aroma and a slightly grainy texture that harmonize particularly well with seafood. In Cagliari, it is therefore often served as Fregola ai frutti di mare, which also reflects the city's proximity to the sea. A very authentic place to sample this specialty is the Lo Zenit restaurant, which is located right by the water. The location, furnishings, and quality of the food are equally inspiring. (© Getty Images) More Culinary Delights: Culurgiones: These are traditional Sardinian dumplings similar to ravioli and filled with potato, cheese, and mint. Delicious culurgiones are available in highly rated vegan restaurant Gintilla, among other places. Porceddu: Slowly grilled over an open fire, suckling pig is a feast for meat lovers – crispy on the outside and as juicy as it is herbal on the inside. The unofficial Sardinian national dish is mainly found in the countryside, but you can also ask for it in traditional restaurants in Cagliari such as the Trattoria Lillicu. Fine dining the Sardinian way: The chefs at the Chiaro Scuro restaurant reinterpret Sardinian produce through a contemporary lens. Seasonal ingredients are carefully arranged and thoughtfully elevated without losing touch with the island's cuisine. The result is modern, refined, and rooted in excellent craftsmanship. The monumental historic fortification Bastione di Saint Remy is one of the most famous sites in Cagliari and offers a wide-ranging view over the city. The large light stone terrace opens onto the Castello district and all the way to the coast. It is most captivating in late afternoon, when the light falls softly over the rooftops and sea. This makes it an easily accessible yet impressive viewpoint from which to experience Cagliari from a different perspective.
