["Learn what happens to your seat reservation if your flight is rebooked or canceled.","Find out what happens to your seat reservation if your flight is cancelled or you have to rebook it. If your flight gets cancelled entirely, or you get rebooked onto another flight on which there is no equivalent seat available in your seat category (e.g. Extra Legroom), you are eligible for a refund of your seat reservation fee. If you get rebooked onto another flight on which there is an equivalent seat available in your seat category (e.g. Extra Legroom), your initial seat reservation remains valid and no refund is needed. In the event of a refund, your reservation fee will be reimbursed onto your initial payment method (e.g. credit card). If the refund is made automatically, you will receive an email confirmation. The airline you booked with is responsible for the refund. If you booked via a third party (e.g. a travel agency), please contact them directly Please note that seat reservations cannot be refunded at the airport or on board. If your seat reservation changes, the following cancellation and refund conditions apply: In the event of an operational change (e.g. change of aircraft type, change of itinerary), we will try to rebook you to the same seat category that you have previously booked. However, the previously selected seat number may vary. If we are unable to rebook you to the same seat category, the seat reservation is refundable. If you have made a seat reservation for your flight and would like to change your seat, you can do this via “My Bookings”. The following rules apply: You can change to a lower seat category free of charge. The price difference is non-refundable, but remains in your reservation and can be used for a later rebooking to a higher seat category (e.g. back to the original seat category, if available, or to a higher seat category). You can rebook to a higher seat category by paying the price difference. If you change or rebook your flight and the route remains the same, you can transfer your seat reservation to the new flight. If the original seat category is not available on the new flight, you can rebook to a lower seat category free of charge. The price difference is non-refundable, but remains in your reservation and can be used for a later rebooking to a higher seat category (e.g. to the originally booked seat category if it becomes available again). you can rebook to a higher seat category by paying the price difference.
