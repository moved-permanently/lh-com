Of course, the boats on Line 62 (part of the public transit system!) don’t go that far, but they do take me all the way to Övelgönne, where I get off and treat myself to a cold drink at the nearby Strandperle kiosk while my bare feet sink into the sand. By the way: the Strandperle is also one of the best spots in Hamburg to watch the sunset. It's not quite time for that yet, though – by now, hunger has taken over. I either walk or hop on a rental bike (in Hamburg, it’s called Stadtrad) along the Elbe, heading toward Altona, where I’ve reserved a table at Maquis.
