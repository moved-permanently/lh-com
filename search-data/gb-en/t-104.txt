Bag drop-off for bulky baggage Surfboards, fully foldable travel pushchairs, and backpacks need to be checked in separately at the bulky-baggage counter. You will be told at the airport whether your item of baggage counts as bulky baggage. Please check in your bulky baggage only after you have checked in your regular baggage. Location of bulky baggage drop-off: Terminal 1 between Departures Halls A and B (between Zones 12 and 13), counters 301–304. Pushchairs and child seats can be dropped off in Departures Hall B at counter 314.
