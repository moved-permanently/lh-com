Rugged Concrete Aesthetics and Neo-Renaissance Mansions On the edge of the Břevnov district stand two monumental concrete towers that are officially ventilation towers for the two-kilometer-long Strahov Tunnel. But I believe these 49-meter giants, with their rugged aesthetics, have the potential to become cultural landmarks of Prague. I photographed them for my \"Normal Is Boring\" series – and Italian singer Damiano David, who won the 2021 Eurovision Song Contest with his band Måneskin, used them as a video shoot location. DSC Gallery is one of Prague's leading venues for contemporary art. Located in a 19th-century city palace, the gallery offers a platform for emerging Czech and international artists. The Villa Pellé gallery is also housed in a stunning building. The Neo-Renaissance mansion once served as the residence of French General Maurice Pellé during the First Republic. It fell into disrepair during the socialist era, was restored in the early 2000s, and has been operated by the NGO Porte Association since 2014. Its bright enfilade rooms now host rotating exhibitions, chamber concerts, and other cultural events.
