["When travelling with children, we recommend reserving seats early. On Allegris aircraft in Business Class, children must sit next to or directly behind an accompanying adult, as seating across the aisle is not permitted.","To ensure suitable seating, we strongly recommend doing a seat reservation as early as possible. Seat reservation is free of charge for children, except for the Allegris Business Class Suite. The adult must sit next to each child or behind. Seating across the aisle or sitting behind a child in Business Suite is not permitted. The child sits next to the adult. For example: Adult in seat 2G and child in seat 2D. Adult in seat 6D and child in seat 6G. The adult sits behind the child. For example: Adult in seat 4D and child in seat 3E. Adult in seat 6C and child in seat 5A. If no suitable combination is available or further information on child seating is required, please contact our Service Centers at the earliest after you have finished your booking. It is forbidden that the adult sits on the other side of the aisle. Furthermore it is not allowed that the adult sits in front of the child. Also it is not allowed that a child is sitting in a Business Suite in row 2 and the adult is sitting behind in row 3. For example: Adult in seat 3K and child in seat 4H. Adult in seat 6C and child in seat 6D. Adult in seat 3E and child in seat 2D. The exemplary seating visualisation shows only some arrangements, more combinations may be possible. In case two children are travelling with two adults each child should be seated with one adult according to the examples above. The adult sits behind the first child and next to the second child. For example: Adult in seat 4D, child 1 in seat 3E and child 2 in seat 4G. Adult in seat 6G, child 1 in seat 5F and child 2 in seat 6D. The adult sits behind the children. For example: Adult in seat 5F, child 1 in seat 4D and child 2 in seat 4G.
