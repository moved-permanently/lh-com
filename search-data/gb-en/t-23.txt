Plus: three insider tips from the Lufthansa Insights Editorial Team I love that you can get out into nature so quickly from my hometown. Every 20 minutes or so, a train leaves from Dublin Connolly Station and goes straight to the Howth Peninsula. After just under half an hour, you're in a different world. In this former fishing village, there's no traffic noise – just the sound of waves and seagulls. The cliff-top trails are perfect for hiking, and the sea shimmers in turquoise. Along the way, you'll find plenty of little bays for swimming. In the evening, I like to stop at one of Howth's traditional pubs – or grab a portion of fish-and-chips at the long-established Beshoff Bros on Harbour Road.
