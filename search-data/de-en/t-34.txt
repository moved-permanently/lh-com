A Library That Brings Everyone Together Oodi, Helsinki's central library, is considered to be one of the best libraries in the world, and its wide range of services is pioneering. You can not only borrow books here, but also test 3D printers, record songs in the music studio, borrow an electric guitar, attend cooking classes, or rent small meeting rooms or a photo studio. Oodi's success is clear: an average of 7,000 people use the library every day. Paradise Is an Island Helsinki's coastline is around 130 kilometers long, as there are over 300 islands within the city limits. Especially during the summer months, people use the island archipelago as a retreat from city life.
