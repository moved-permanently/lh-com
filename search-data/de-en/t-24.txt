Immerse yourself in the authentic heart of the city.","Flight attendant Farah Benkassem recommends a day trip to the Valley of the Butterflies High up in the Sierra Nevada, about a three-hour drive from Mexico City, lies the Valley of the Butterflies. Every year in the fall, millions of butterflies fly here from North America to spend the winter. When the temperatures rise a few months later, the beautiful creatures fly around the valley in their thousands. All you have to do is hold out your hand and they will sit on it. The best months to see this natural phenomenon are October, February and March. You start in Senguio, from where a beautiful four-kilometer trail leads to the Monarch Butterfly Sanctuary Sierra Chincua.
