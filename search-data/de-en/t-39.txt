Zurich Ticks by This Clock The clock tower of St. Peter Church has the largest church clock face in Europe, measuring 8.7 meters across – and for centuries, it was the city's official timekeeper. All public clocks had to follow its lead. The minute hand stretches an impressive 5.5 meters and weighs 94 kilos; the hour hand comes in just under five meters and 74 kilos. Today, the clock runs digitally, but the distinct \"jerk\" of the hand moving 45 centimeters each minute remains unmistakable.
