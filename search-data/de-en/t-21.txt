The selection is tempting, but I usually opt for a croissant – which is not, as many people think, the Viennese version of the French pastry, but rather its predecessor. You could say that the croissant was invented in Vienna. 10:30 AM: Enjoy the Sun in the Stadtpark I take the croissant and another coffee to go and retire to the Stadtpark to enjoy the sun, which is of course shining on my perfect Vienna day.
