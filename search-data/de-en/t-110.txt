Once payment has been made, the bag charge cannot be refunded or offset separately. If ticket conditions allow a refund, the service can be refunded or changed accordingly. In case of an involuntary ticket change or refund, the service can be refunded or changed. In case of a fare upgrade to a higher fare, bag charge can be refunded. Purchase of bag is restricted up to five bags. In case, more bags are needed, customer must contact the service center or to purchase additional items of baggage at the airport.
