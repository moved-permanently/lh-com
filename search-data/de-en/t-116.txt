["","You are entitled to a refund of Air Passenger Duty United Kingdom under certain conditions (Air Passenger Duty United Kingdom). UK Air passenger Duty (APD) has been abolished for children aged 2 to 11 years when travelling in Economy class, on all flights departing from UK airports on or after May 1st, 2015. Our booking system has already been updated with this information and no Air Passenger Duty will be charged. In addition to this, UK Air Passenger Duty has been abolished for young persons aged between 12 and 15 years when travelling in Economy Class, on all flights departing the UK on or after March 1st, 2016. APD still applies to all bookings in Business and First Class. We are currently in the process of updating our systems so that our website in the future will display the correct APD for young persons aged 12 to 15 years travelling in Economy class from the March 1st, 2016 onwards. While we are working on the system changes, we ask that when making an Economy class booking for young passengers aged 12 to 15 years for travel March 1st, 2016 onwards from the UK, that you contact our Service centre to make the reservation from the UK. Contact Service centre If you have already made a booking on lufthansa.com, we will still refund the APD for young persons aged 12 to 15 years for travel March 1st, 2016 onwards.
