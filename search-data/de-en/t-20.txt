It usually costs no more than 80 or 100 euros, which is a small price to pay for a safe and comfortable way to explore the city and its surroundings. It’s difficult to navigate the traffic chaos of Kenya’s capital city, home to over 4.5 million people, using public transportation. Giraffes, buffalo, and zebras: Kenya’s first national park, located just outside Nairobi, is home to an impressive variety of wildlife (©Mauritius; header image © Getty Images) Kenya’s first national park is home to an impressive variety of wildlife, including monkeys, lions, buffalo, and zebras. Sometimes, you can even see giraffes walking against the city skyline, which is a truly unusual sight. Set aside two to three hours to experience the park's unique atmosphere and see as many animals as possible. It's an experience you won't want to miss! The last time I was in Nairobi, I had lunch at the Tin Roof Café in the Karen neighborhood, a green oasis on the far west side of the city, surrounded by tall trees and palms.
