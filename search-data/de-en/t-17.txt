That spirit still lingers in Malasaña today. For a late lunch—typical in Spain—I head back south to Plaza Mayor. There’s always something going on there: street performers breathing fire, making giant soap bubbles, or juggling—and lots of tourists. I don’t eat at one of the (often overpriced) restaurants, though. Instead, I grab some classic street food from a nearby alley: bocadillo de calamares—white bread with fried calamari rings.
