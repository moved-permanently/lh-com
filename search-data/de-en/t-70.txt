Other plans? Verboten! Germany’s 13,000 kilometers of autobahn may be a magnet for car lovers, but don’t floor the gas pedal just yet. Contrary to popular belief, at least one third of the legendary highways has speed limits, and between construction zones and traffic jams, safe stretches of truly open road are rare. Germans usually only reserve their spots before breakfast already when on holiday in Majorca. But as soon as an important European Championship match is on, the towels – er, jackets – are also laid out very early on the public screening seats back home in Germany. If there’s a saucer at the restroom entrance in a stadium or club, it’s not a cup that’s missing. You leave a tip there for the restroom attendants. Wearing their white coats, they are as Deutsch as a German shepherd: anyone who doesn’t pay (at least 50 cents) is barked at. Getting bread rolls, buying a soft drink, ordering a kebab – yes, feel free, but please don’t try to pay by card. Germans have a close relationship with cash. Use the Flight as a Time-out and Arrive Relaxed Getting up early, long flight times, perhaps a change of planes in between: traveling can be exhausting. That's why I use the time on the plane to relax. I enjoy offline time. I listen to music, watch a movie, and write in my diary. The good thing is that Lufthansa Allegris offers many options to customize the travel experience to individual needs. When I'm tired, I turn my seat into the 2.20-meter-long bed and stretch out. If I do need to do some work, I have enough space for that too. If I'm traveling with my wife, I can book the double seat, for example, where the center console can be lowered. So everyone can create their own personal comfort zone. 3. Travel More Relaxed Thanks to Light Luggage When I travel alone, I make sure that my hand luggage is rather small. However, I always have a few things with me: headphones to listen to music, my diary, and something to read. Even though I like to be offline on board, I don't travel without my laptop. Sometimes I have to catch up on a few emails or do other work-related things. So I keep it simple: I only pack what I really need to make the trip relaxed and uncomplicated. Thanks to Lufthansa Allegris, I will be able to travel even lighter in future. Because there are options on board for charging mobile devices, and there are noise-canceling headphones at my seat. Light and tasty: Dirk Nowitzki recommends not eating too heavy on flights (© Yves Bachmann) 4. Opt for Light Meals I make sure I don't eat too heavily on long flights. I prefer healthy snacks that satisfy my hunger without weighing me down. There is a good selection on board Lufthansa: balanced meals that are light and taste good. And when we're traveling with the kids, it's of course great if we don't have to take a lot with us, but if everything is taken care of during the flight.
