Famous for its glamorous casino: Monte Carlo (© Getty Images) Named after a classic film: the African Queen restaurant (© Cedou and Claire Israel) An elegant gem on the Mediterranean: Beaulieu-sur-Mer (© Shutterstock) The atmosphere at African Queen is super relaxed – that's why I like the restaurant so much. And of course because of the incredibly good food. I especially like to order the Caesar salad or salmon with sorrel (saumon à l'oseille). While I eat, I watch the sun slowly set and bathe the harbor in warm light. This is where my day ends – with the feeling of being in exactly the right place. In addition to the unique attitude to life, she particularly appreciates the fact that there is a fantastic view of the sea in so many places. A brief stop at the Picasso Museum in Antibes: If you’re already in Antibes Juan-les- Pins, pay a visit to the Musée Picasso at Château Grimaldi. Picasso worked there for several weeks in 1946; the château later became the Picasso Museum and still displays works that the artist left to the city. Panoramic view in Èze: On the journey between Nice and Monaco, it’s worth stopping in the mountain village of Èze. The Jardin Exotique is situated among the ruins of a medieval fortress, 429 meters (1,407 feet) above the Mediterranean Sea, and offers one of the most spectacular panoramic views on the Riviera. Garden art at Cap Ferrat: Before dinner in Beaulieu-sur-Mer, it’s worth visiting the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild in Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat. This Belle Époque villa is located between Nice and Monaco, overlooks the Mediterranean Sea, and is surrounded by nine themed gardens, ranging from French to Japanese, Provençal to exotic.
