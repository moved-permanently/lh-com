Restrictions apply with regard to the refundability of the fare purchased. Depending on the fare conditions and any segments of the ticket used, the refund value of the fare, taxes and fees will be calculated and refunded to the original method of payment. You can find out whether your ticket is refundable, and under which conditions, in your booking confirmation email under the item “Fare conditions”. You can cancel your trip and request a refund under My bookings up to 2 hours before departure. My bookings To log in, enter your surname and booking code. Click on the “Cancel/refund”button next to the relevant booking and confirm your selection on the following page. If your booking contains more than one passenger, you can choose which passenger(s) you’d like to cancel. When you cancel for selected passengers, your booking will be split: the passengers who still wish to travel will keep their original booking and booking code. After confirming your selection and getting an overview of your refund amount, your flight booking will be cancelled and you will receive an automatic confirmation email.
