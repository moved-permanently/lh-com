A stone’s throw from Rue Saint-Honoré is Place Vendôme, known for its elegant buildings and a Parisian icon: Coco Chanel. She lived in the Ritz Hotel on Place Vendôme for over 20 years until her death. By the way, you can also book the designer’s suite – for a mere 36,000 euros per night.
