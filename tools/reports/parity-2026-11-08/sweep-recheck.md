## Method

- **Ours:** each page on its brand host in headless Chrome, one page at a time, 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, device scale factor 1, with the TAP sweep's settle method (sweep-tap.md): the DOM and the load event, no new request for 1.5 s, the consent accepted and the showcase notice acknowledged, scrolled to the bottom in 0.8-viewport steps of 350 ms, pictures complete, fonts ready, back to the top, 800 ms; a full-page PNG. All 554 answered 200. www's 390 shots in the 002/003 archives come from a resize after the 1440 load (their README); ours load fresh at each width.
- **Height:** www's recorded scrollHeight against ours.
- **Pixels:** pixelmatch (threshold 0.1, as the TAP sweep) over the area both screenshots have; in brackets the earlier brand sweeps' measure (pixels whose RGB differs by more than 30), comparable with sweep-austrian-brussels.md and sweep-brands.md.
- **Headings:** www's recorded h1/h2 matched to ours by text; "jump" is where neighbouring headings part most (where a cause sits).
- **Pictures:** the archives hold no images; www's are the image files in the recorded page (its shadow roots included, AEM's .transform/ renditions by their original file), matched to ours by alt text; missing: www's files whose alt text ours does not have.
- **Score** (the order): pixels % + |height %| (at most 100).
