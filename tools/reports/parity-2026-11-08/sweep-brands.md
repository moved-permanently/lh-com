## How it was measured

- **Ours:** each page on its brand host, one page at a time, in headless Chrome at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844 (device
  scale factor 1).
  - The capture's settle method: the load event; no new request for 1.5 s; scrolled to the bottom in 0.8-viewport
    steps of 350 ms; all images complete; fonts ready; back to the top.
  - The showcase notice acknowledged; the cookie dialog accepted (Accept all) once per brand.
  - A full-page PNG clipped to the width.
  - Recorded: scrollHeight; every h1 and h2 outside the header and footer (text, box, size); each block's box; every
    visible picture in main of 40 px or more; the header and the footer.
- **Path mapping:** Cargo takes the replica paths from its README: the homepages at `/<ll>/home`; Support & Contact
  and the feedback form in English (`/en/contact-us?reason=…`); the article at `/<ll>/news/142-…`. Air Dolomiti takes
  `/`, `/en/`, `/de/` for .it, .eu, .de. The rest use www's paths.
- **The comparison** (per page and width):
  - **Page height**, www's scrollHeight against ours.
  - **h1 and h2:** www's measured headings matched to ours by text; the h1's y and the largest offset.
  - **Each block's top:** each of our blocks against the nearest top www has there (its sections on Cargo and
    B2B, its headlines and h1 to h3 on all). The block furthest from any is listed.
  - **Picture sizes:** the count and the largest picture on each side. The archives hold no images, so www's
    pictures were measured on the archive rendered offline (our own server on 127.0.0.1, every other host
    unresolvable). Only pictures whose box www's CSS sizes count there, so www's counts are a lower bound.
  - **The pixel difference:** both PNGs at a quarter of their size, the shorter one padded white, pixelmatch at
    threshold 0.15. Measured over the whole page and over the first screen.
- **Score** (the order): the larger of the full-page pixel difference and the height difference, both in %. The
  pixel difference counts every changed picture, price and news teaser, so it is high everywhere (15 to 70 %): read
  it with the height and headings.
- **"After the fix"**: the page measured the same way on main--<site>--moved-permanently.aem.page after the push and
  the uploads. The brand hosts serve the published documents, so a changed document shows there only once it is
  published. The code is on the brand hosts already, and the brand host gives the same figure where the fix is code
  (discover.turnsthe.page's check-in at the airport at 390: 1810 px, was 4134).
