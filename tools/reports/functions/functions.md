## Notes

1. Air Dolomiti sells through its own booking engine, not Lufthansa's shop. Since 1 November the search form is on the replica's homepages, route pages and booking pages and hands the trip to that engine, as www does; the engine's bot check can show a captcha, as it can on www.
2. Lufthansa's manage booking and hotel tabs hand over to two services that run in AEM today. Their replacements answer with test data, and since 1 November the tabs post to them on the airline's own address; on the replica's addresses they keep opening www, so no real booking reaches test data.
3. On Discover, ITA, Austrian and Brussels Airlines, manage booking goes through a deep link to the login and profile pages, which are on the new platform since 1 November. The services behind the deep link answer with test data, and since 1 November the replicas' addresses route the deep link to them, so it ends on the replica's login page.
4. Switching off Air Dolomiti's current website is a goal, so its timetable, manage booking, newsletter and eSIM pages get versions of their own. Its booking service answers our addresses, so timetable and manage booking can work as on www; the newsletter becomes a form on our own forms service.
5. Cargo's booking, schedules, customer portal, station finder and eServices stay on Cargo's current systems; the new site links to them.
6. Air Dolomiti's eSIM shop (from the vendor Breeze) is built into the replica, but its switch stays off: Breeze answers every call from our address with 403, because the widget's key belongs to Air Dolomiti's site. It works once Air Dolomiti or Breeze allows our address.
7. The help center, culinary hub and compensation claim apps are in the shared code and load on our pages, but their data comes from the airline's Travel ID service (and, for the claim, Miles & More's booking service), which refuses our test addresses. They work once the site runs on the airline's own address, or once Lufthansa allows ours.
8. Austrian's chat assistant is loaded from Austrian's website, outside AEM. Since 1 November its button shows on the 213 pages that have it on www, in English, German, French, Spanish and Italian.
9. Air Dolomiti's chat assistant is built into the replica, but its switch stays off: the chat vendor sends no chat to our address until Air Dolomiti or the vendor adds a rule for it.
10. Brussels Airlines' chat assistant loads from Brussels Airlines' website, outside AEM, as Austrian's does. Since 1 November its button shows on the pages that have it on www, in all six languages.
11. Cargo's newsletter form loads from Lufthansa Cargo's Microsoft Dynamics, which takes sign-ups only from sites Cargo allows. Lufthansa Cargo has to allow our address for form hosting in its Dynamics settings; until then the embed stays off and the page links to Cargo's own newsletter page.
12. Personal content for signed-in customers needs Lufthansa: Travel ID, the group's login, accepts only the airline's own website as the address to return to and as the site allowed to call it. The replicas can't sign anyone in until they run on the airline's address, or Lufthansa allows ours.

## Functions

- **Flight search**: The search form on the homepage and booking pages that sends travellers to the airline's booking shop with route, dates and travellers.
- **Travellers and cabin picker**: The part of the flight search where travellers choose how many adults, children and infants fly, and in which cabin.
- **Flight status**: The form that finds a flight's current status by flight number, route or airport.
- **Online check-in**: The form that takes a booking code and name and opens the airline's check-in app.
- **Manage booking**: Where travellers look up an existing booking to change it or add services.
- **Timetable / schedule search**: A search for scheduled flights on a route, where the airline has one apart from flight status.
- **Hotel search tab**: A tab in the flight search that hands a hotel search over to a partner.
- **Car rental**: Car rental booking, as an app on the page or as a link to a rental partner.
- **Trip management app**: Lufthansa's app on the website for upcoming trips: booking, trip details and help.
- **Lounge finder**: The search for lounges by airport and travel class or status.
- **Security waiting times**: Current waiting times at airport security checks, by airport.
- **Check-in deadline search**: The search that shows the check-in deadline at an airport.
- **Group booking**: How groups of ten or more travellers book: a request form, a portal or a link.
- **eSIM shop**: A partner shop for mobile data abroad (eSIM).
- **Fare teaser (live prices)**: Teasers for fixed routes that show today's lowest fare from the airline's price service.
- **Destination finder (homepage slider)**: The homepage slider of destinations with live prices from the traveller's departure airport.
- **Destination finder grid (offers page)**: The offers page with filters and a grid of destinations with live prices.
- **Offers with fixed prices (route cards)**: Route cards whose prices are typed in by authors instead of loaded live.
- **New destinations map**: A map with pins for destinations.
- **Homepage stage countdown**: A countdown in the homepage's main teaser for an offer that ends soon.
- **Flash teaser / Message Center**: Short notices about disruptions or news at the top of pages, with a page of current travel information.
- **Culinary hub**: The app for viewing and pre-ordering meals for a booked flight.
- **eJournals**: Free newspapers and magazines that booked travellers download with their booking code or ticket number.
- **Newsroom**: Press releases and news for journalists.
- **Site search**: The search field and results page for the website's own pages.
- **Help and contact app**: The help center app that searches help topics and leads to the right contact.
- **Live chat and digital assistant**: Chat with a digital assistant or an agent.
- **Forms**: Contact, feedback and request forms that send their entries by mail.
- **Claims form**: The form for compensation and refund claims after a disruption.
- **Newsletter sign-up**: Signing up for the airline's newsletter.
- **Login, My flights, cart**: Signing in with the airline's account, and the pages for signed-in travellers: their flights and the shopping cart.
- **Personalised content**: Parts of pages that only signed-in travellers see, such as personal offers.
- **Country / language selector**: The dialog to switch country and language.
- **Cookie consent**: The banner and settings that ask visitors which cookies and tags they allow.
- **Accessibility toolbar**: Controls in the header for contrast and text size.
- **Shipment tracking**: Tracking a cargo shipment by its air waybill number.
- **eBooking quick start**: The start of Cargo's freight booking: origin and destination, then the booking portal after login.
- **Station finder**: Cargo's map and details of its stations.
- **eServices (DGR, AWB stock, rates, ...)**: Cargo's customer services, most behind login: dangerous goods, air waybill stock, rates, claims and more.
