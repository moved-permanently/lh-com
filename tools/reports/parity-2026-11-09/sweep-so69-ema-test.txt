How each batch is swept
-----------------------
- A batch's documents: the READY rows of tools/reports/parity-2026-11-09/delta-so60-switch-NN.txt less the documents live still has
  marked. The state commits ("so60 switch batch NN: n documents collapsed") give counts only, so tools/importer/switch-census.mjs
  --batch <list> --env live (main--lh-com--moved-permanently.aem.live, 3 requests at a time) names them: a document with markers on
  live is held or skipped and not swept. The census also says when a batch is on live: polled every 4 minutes, the batch counts as
  live when at most a tenth of its documents still read marked.
- Host lufthansa.turnsthe.page, widths 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, Chrome headless shell 1254 with unhinted text, consent and the
  showcase notice stored, at most 3 pages at once. Each page-width settles as order 79's: load, lazy pictures eager, one scroll,
  network idle (8 s cap), every section and block loaded, every drawn own picture complete, the height the same on 3 reads 500 ms
  apart (15 s cap). Requests off our host (www.*, lh-proxy, airline hosts, third parties) are refused and counted, never sent;
  pictures from them do not count.
- Checks per page-width (logs/p4/so69/sw69.cjs, order 79's kinds): a broken own-host picture (complete, no pixels); sideways scroll;
  a section or block never loaded; consecutive blocks drawn over each other; a cut-off label (a button, link button, label, tab,
  summary or heading whose text is wider than its clipping box); text out of its block; an empty block; and a collision pass: the
  first and last line of every text node in main, at its centre and 3 px inside both ends, hit-tested below the sticky header. A hit
  on anything but the text's own element, an ancestor or a descendant is a collision; fixed elements (the demo badge, the stage bars
  fixed at the viewport's foot) and stretched card links (a link whose ::after covers its card) are not, nor text inside a closed
  details. Form fields over their own floating labels are counted apart (DESIGN).
- Each page against its own marked copy as rendered before its batch: the batches' documents were rendered on live from 01:11 CET,
  before any of batches 22-26 was published (heights; logs/p4/so69/pre-NN.jsonl). More than 15 % off at either width is a finding,
  read again alone.
- Kinds: FAULT = drawn wrong. PROPOSED = a rule for ema-adobe (lufthansa.com's theme and the shared files are theirs; not edited here).
  ROW = a source fix in delta-so69-com-faults.txt. LISTED = not proven, or fewer than 3 documents with no row. CONTENT = the
  document's own. DESIGN = drawn so on purpose. FUNCTION = an app or a live answer the refused hosts withhold. ARTEFACT = the check.
- Scratch (not in git): logs/p4/so69 (sw69.cjs, sum69.py, post.sh, the census and sweep outputs).
