Item 3: the empty POI map boxes
-------------------------------
Cause: ours (blocks/poi-map/poi-map.js).
- The block draws its 500 px view as soon as it is decorated. It looks up its points only when it comes within 300 px of the
  viewport (whenNear, an IntersectionObserver).
- A map whose points are not recorded is hidden only when that lookup comes back empty. On our hosts the recordings index is
  the only source for pointsOfInterest, and the labels call to www is refused, retried, then read from recordings.
- So the box's fate depends on the scroll. A load whose section showed only after the measurement scrolled back to the top
  never came near: an empty, busy 500 px box. A load that passed it: the box gone.
- /xx/es/first-class: neither of its two maps is recorded; 4 loads at 1440 gave 24,562 once and 23,514 three times (the
  +-1,000 px of order 70). /xx/es/book-and-manage/stopover/neuschwanstein at 390: 3 of 3 loads kept the busy box (3,973 px).
- For a visitor, the box stood empty until they scrolled near and the lookup ended, then the page jumped up by 500 px.
  Checked on the served page: neuschwanstein at 390, the map scrolled into view, hidden within 1 s (3,973 -> 3,441 px).
- The maps with recorded points (gb/en, de/en, de/de, at/de first-class) draw in every load; our renders refuse the OSM tiles,
  so the sweep reads them as "empty" too.

Fix: on lufthansa.com (body.brand-lufthansa; not TAP's sheet maps, not maps with point rows) the points are looked up at once
and only the drawing waits for the viewport. A map without points is hidden from the start, whatever the scroll.
