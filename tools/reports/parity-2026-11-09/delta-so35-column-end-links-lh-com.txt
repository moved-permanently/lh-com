# Standing order 35 (2026-11-16), item 3: a column view's end mark before its links (datenschutz 1440, 3.5 -> 6.5 %
# since 11-09, the column marks of 11-12).
# Cause, located: www keeps the standalone links of a column view inside the view (datenschutz: the headline, the text
# and six maui-navigation-links in one onecolumn column-view-item, lh-com bd-snapshot). The importer's placeColumnMarks
# (tools/importer/transformers/lufthansa-cleanup.js) counts only p, headings, lists, tables and media as a view's
# leaves. When it runs, the links are still bare <a> in divs (convertMauiLinks has made them links; the paragraphs
# around them come later). So "{column-end...}" lands on the text before the links: the view's end padding (20 / 40px)
# draws between the text and the first link, and none after the last link. datenschutz: the first link 67px under
# the paragraph's top (www 49), 16px lower.
# Reach (published copies of 11-14, logs/b613/pub): 719 column ends right before link-only paragraphs in lh-com's
# documents. www's snapshot has 278 of them in the same view, 5 in another view; 415 have no snapshot and 21 could not
# be matched. 44 of those documents are recorded.
# Fix: per document, the end tokens (column-end, column-end-outer, column-spaced) leave the paragraph and an own
# "{tokens}" paragraph follows the last link. Other tokens (full-width, column-start..., copytext-small) stay where they
# are. This is the form the importer writes when a view ends on something that cannot hold a mark.
