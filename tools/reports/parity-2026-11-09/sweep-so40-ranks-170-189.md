## The Brussels footer rule (drafted, HOLD)

**Draft** (.scratch, not in the theme):

    @media (width < 768px) { body.brand-brussels .footer .footer-legal { max-width: calc((100% + 20px) * 0.75 - 20px); } }

That is www's col-9 of the footer row less its 10 px padding each side: 254.5 px of links at 390. The theme already keeps the legal row to three quarters from 1025 px ("the French row wraps its sixth link, as www's"). Phones had no such limit.
