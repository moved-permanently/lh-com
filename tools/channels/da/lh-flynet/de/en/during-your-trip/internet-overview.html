Our internet options at a glance

To keep you connected throughout your flight, one of our two internet offers awaits you on board: the new Lufthansa Group Wi-Fi powered by Starlink or our FlyNet® service.

Lufthansa Group Wi-Fi

With our new complimentary high-speed internet, you'll enjoy a fast and powerful connection. Whether you're working, sending messages, or watching a movie, Lufthansa Group Wi-Fi keeps you connected in every travel class. With a connection that stays with you seamlessly from gate to destination.

Lufthansa Group Wi-Fi is currently available on selected flights and is gradually being rolled out across our entire fleet.

FlyNet®

FlyNet® is our trusted onboard internet service, keeping you reliably connected throughout your journey. Explore the complimentary FlyNet® portal and stay in touch with your loved ones through Free Messaging. Or choose a FlyNet® internet package for browsing, working, and entertainment that is entirely tailored to your needs.
